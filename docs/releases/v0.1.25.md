# QBRemote 0.1.25（26）更新说明

## 中文

- 种子列表每行新增已上传 / 已下载总量（图标显示），去掉了与之重复的大小。
- 种子详情页新增已上传、已下载数据。
- 服务器卡片上的状态（上传中、下载中、暂停中、错误、校验中、等待中、总种子）可直接点击，跳转到对应状态的种子列表，数量与列表一致。
- 修复种子列表状态筛选：qBittorrent 5.x 的"已暂停"筛选为空、"下载中"漏掉停滞的下载、Transmission 校验中的种子未被统计。
- 每日上传量统计更准确：跨零点按时间比例拆分，不再丢失夜间的上传；开启下载完成通知后，app 关闭时也会在后台持续统计。
- 上传分布地图统计更准确：两次采样之间新连接的用户也会计入。
- 修复种子数量超过 500 时上传统计在重启后被清空的问题。

## English

- Torrent list rows show lifetime uploaded / downloaded totals (icons), replacing the duplicate size.
- Torrent details show uploaded and downloaded totals.
- Status pills on the server card (Uploading, Downloading, Paused, Error, Checking, Queued, Total) open the torrent list filtered to exactly those torrents.
- Fixed list status filters: "Paused" was empty on qBittorrent 5.x, "Downloading" missed stalled downloads, and verifying Transmission torrents were not counted.
- More accurate daily upload: the overnight gap is split at midnight instead of lost, and with completion notifications on, counting continues in the background while the app is closed.
- More accurate upload map: peers that connected between two samples are now counted.
- Fixed upload stats resetting on restart for libraries with more than 500 torrents.
